This policy covers the mobile game *Glow Garden* (Işık Bahçesi, Android package com.wandaiser.glowgarden) developed by Wandaiser.

## Information we collect

The game does not ask you to create an account, and we as the developer **do not collect personal information**. Your game progress (garden, plants, settings) is stored **only on your device** and is never sent to us.

## Third-party services

The game uses the following Google services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

- **Google AdMob (advertising):** To show optional rewarded ads, AdMob may collect data such as device identifiers (e.g. the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and device and app interaction information, for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent for ads, and can change this choice at any time under *Settings > Ad privacy options* in the game.
  Google's policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy> — How Google uses data from partner sites: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- **Google Play Billing (in-app purchases):** Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; the game only uses the purchase information from Google Play to confirm a purchase.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Data deletion

Because your game data is stored only on your device, it is deleted when you uninstall the game. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's *Settings > Google > Ads*.

## Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the date on this page.
