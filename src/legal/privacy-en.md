## This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, ads or tracking, and has no accounts or forms. Two preferences are stored only in your own browser and are never sent to us: whether sound is on, and whether you have already seen the intro animation in this session.

The site is served as static files. Like any web server, our hosting provider may keep short-term technical logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Links to Google Play and other sites are governed by those sites' own policies.
